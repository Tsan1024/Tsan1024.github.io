<section lang="en" aria-label="English introduction">

NoNap is a lightweight macOS menu-bar and Windows tray app that keeps your laptop running with the lid closed—ideal for builds, downloads, model training, and AI agents. It includes a countdown timer, a low-battery cutoff, and an English / Simplified Chinese interface. No account required.

</section>
